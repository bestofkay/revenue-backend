# User Guide

## Revenue officers

1. Sign in to the admin portal
2. Create an assessment with payer details and revenue lines
3. Submit for approval
4. After approval, generate an invoice (auto-creates payment link)
5. Share the pay URL via SMS, email, WhatsApp, or QR/PDF

## Approvers

1. Open Assessments
2. Review line items and payer information
3. Approve or reject with comments
4. Multi-step workflows require each configured role in sequence

## Payers (citizens / corporates)

1. Open the payment link (example: `https://pay.domain.gov.ng/pay/NCS202607000001`)
2. Confirm revenue name, amount, invoice number, bank, virtual account, expiry, status
3. Pay via bank transfer to the virtual account, or card/USSD/QR when offered
4. Keep the electronic receipt (QR verification + digital signature)

## Treasury officers

1. Review Settlements for pending items
2. Create a settlement batch
3. After TSA confirmation, mark the batch settled with the TSA reference
